We Built the Tool
We Wished Existed.

Comparix was born from frustration — spending hours comparing prices across multiple tabs, only to find a better deal after checkout. We fixed that.

Our Story

In 2023, engineers and designers — all tired of being overcharged — built Comparix. The idea: give every shopper real-time price data across every platform in one place.

We spent 18 months training our price-matching AI on millions of listings. Today, Comparix tracks 12 million+ products across 500+ platforms, saving users an average of ₹1,200 per purchase.

Our mission is radical price transparency. Nobody should overpay simply because they didn't know a better deal was 3 tabs away.
